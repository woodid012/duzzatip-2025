Steal of the draft

Lachlan McAndrew, pick 117 (round 15) by Feathers and noodle soup — finished the #3 most valuable player of all 144 drafted (804 pts as a Ruck). Honourable mentions: Luke Davies-Uniacke at pick 130 (Quack, #18 overall — the best single value-vs-slot pick of the draft), Jai Newcombe at pick 138 (Sharky's, #34), Hugo Garcia at pick 116 (Pinga, #16).

Bust of the draft

Sam Darcy, pick 3 overall by String Theory — finished ranked 130th of 144 (146 pts). The biggest bust by raw rank-gap and by earliness-weighting. Also feeling it: Jesse Hogan (pick 12, rank 133, Feathers) and Errol Gulden (pick 27, rank 118, Miguel — injury did most of that damage).

MVP validation

Max Gawn was the #1 pick and finished the #1 most valuable player (969 pts, Ruck) for Nightmare of Milky Briz. Sometimes the obvious pick is just right. Nick Daicos at pick 4 finished #2 (844) for Pinga — the top of the draft mostly delivered, pick 3 aside.

The cursed ruck stocks

Randy's season died in the ruck: Peter Ladhams missed 17 of Sydney's 19 games, Nicholas Madden 13 of 19, Nick Bryan 9 of 20. For a team named Randy's Ruckin Roalercoaster, the football gods have a sense of humour.

Two kinds of injury pain

Pinga had the healthiest squad overall but the most selection-night ambushes — 7 named starters didn't play, most in the league. The auto-sub engine bailed them out for +211 points of DNP cover (+310 including score swaps), the best rescue job of the season.

Costliest single absences

Errol Gulden (Miguel) — 12 games missed at ~32 a game. Tom Liberatore (Miguel) — 11 at ~32. Will Day (Quack) — 11 at ~29. James Worpel (Feathers) — 11 at ~29. Miguel copping the top two explains a lot about sixth place.

Biggest overachiever

Sharky's Bite — 4th on the ladder, 7th on power. Only 7 of their 21 weeks beat the field median, but the league's best dead-cert record (+300, no negative weeks) is worth ~6 points a round over the worst tipper, and they banked +3 wins of pure fixture luck. Finals on tipping and timing.

Biggest underachiever

Nightmare of Milky Briz — the #1 power team finished 3rd. Top-3 in draft, coaching, health and #1 in scoring, undone by 0–3 in close games and five crabs. On substance they enter September as the premiership favourite, not the third seed. Pinga wears the same tag with a sadder ending: #1 draft, #1 health, 4th on power, no finals.

The conversion story

Feathers turned the 5th-best draft into the minor premiership through coaching (#1) and dead certs (#2). Quack did the reverse: the 2nd-best draft class fell to 7th — the biggest talent-to-ladder drop in the league. What you draft matters less than what you do with it every Thursday night.

Method. All data pulled from the league database via the DuzzaTip API, rounds 1–21 (round 0 excluded: no head-to-head matches and an unreconcilable stats snapshot). Scoring formulas were reimplemented from the app's own rules and validated against 1,007 stored position scores with zero mismatches. Draft value = each player's full-season total at his best-scoring position; value-vs-slot compares each pick against the average of the eight picks in the same draft round. Perfect team = the submitted lineup (including automatic bench cover) matched the optimal assignment of the nine fielded players to the six scoring positions. Injury burden = games missed while the player's club played, weighted by the player's average score when he did play, summed over the current 18-man squad (mid-season trades are attributed to the current owner).
